Questions

Which audio interfaces work?

Any class-compliant UAC1/UAC2 USB audio interface. Some interfaces need to be set to class-compliant mode; vendor-specific modes are not supported.

Why is my take out of sync?

The app timestamps when audio and video actually start and aligns them on export automatically. If you still notice drift over long takes, check that your interface is locked to 48 kHz.

Can I hear the stereo effects from my multi-effects unit?

Yes. Record inputs 1+2 in stereo and enable headphone monitoring: input 1 goes left and input 2 goes right. Use the monitor pan control to fine-tune the balance.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Recordings stay on your device. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. See the privacy policy for details.

What Android version do I need?

Android 10 or newer.
